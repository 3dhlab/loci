# Hosted walkthrough media

`hosted-walkthrough.mp4` is a 64.5-second, 1920×1080, 30 fps screen recording of the published Loci research collection at [loci.threedeezy.com](https://loci.threedeezy.com/public). The recording was captured on 2026-10-04 in America/Chicago. The application stays visible throughout as the viewer browses the collection, opens Field Museum Spoon, rotates the model, selects the Woman’s Face Design annotation, plays its linked video, seeks the transcript, copies a share link, and reopens the shared moment. A fixed Times New Roman banner animates the short instructions. The video uses two short excerpts of original recording audio synchronized to the browser playback clock and retains the source video's captions. The sound follows the two demonstrated playback excerpts.
