# Hosted Loci walkthrough

[Watch the complete 50.1-second walkthrough](hosted-walkthrough.mp4) · [Find the hosted collection link](../../README.md#hosted-research-instance)

The example follows **Field Museum Spoon** from the LIB 2025 VR Object Interpretations collection. It shows published research content, 3D annotations, synchronized video and transcript navigation, link copying, and citation formats. Two short excerpts of the original interview audio play during the matching video passages. The original recording’s visible captions and credits are retained.

## Steps shown

- **0.0–4.2 seconds:** Explore the hosted collection.
- **4.2–8.2 seconds:** Open an object.
- **8.2–22.4 seconds:** Rotate the model, select an annotation, and play its interpretation.
- **22.4–25.8 seconds:** Switch to Focus view.
- **25.8–35.0 seconds:** Select a transcript line and watch its matching captioned passage.
- **35.0–39.5 seconds:** Copy an evidence link.
- **39.5–44.8 seconds:** Choose and copy a citation.
- **44.8–50.1 seconds:** Explore the prototype invitation and animated Loci mark.

The cursor moves continuously between actions and clears the player during the transcript passage. The application remains visible beneath the closing treatment: the homepage’s converge mark animates above the invitation on a light panel over a softly blurred, paused interface.
