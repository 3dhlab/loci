# Sample assets and artwork

The demonstration includes a cube built from numerical coordinates, a silent video with three colored sections, posters, sample transcript text and annotations. The generation script is in `apps/api/app/scripts/generate_demo_assets.py`. Browser tests include generated model, video and poster files.
