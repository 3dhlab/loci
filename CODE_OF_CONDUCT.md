# Community conduct

Discuss code and ideas respectfully, assume good faith, and make critique specific and actionable. Welcome contributors across backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, threats, discriminatory behavior, personal attacks and sharing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict participation to protect the community. Email Craig Stevens at craig.stevens@austin.utexas.edu for private conduct reports. Maintainers should acknowledge reports, review evidence confidentially, consider conflicts of interest, and communicate an appropriate resolution to affected parties. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests and project-managed discussion spaces.
